How to Use
A quick guide to getting the most out of this accounting system.
📦 Stock Purchases
1
Create a new shipment (2 steps)
Click + Import Shipment or + Local Purchase. Step 1 covers costs — supplier, dates, FX transfers, freight, other charges, customs tax. Step 2 covers products. Use Save Draft to save without products yet.
2
FX conversion (imports only)
Enter the USD invoice amount and SGD you actually transferred. The blended FX rate is calculated automatically across multiple transfers, capturing bank fees accurately.
3
Add products — existing or new
Pick from the dropdown (existing store products) or tick "New product" to create a product in the store at the same time. You can also Import Excel using the product template — cases and USD/box cost pre-fill automatically.
4
New products go to store hidden
When you add a new product via a shipment, it is created in the store as hidden. Toggle "Show in Store" in the new product fields to make it visible on Apply to Stock, or enable it later via the Products panel.
5
Apply to Stock when goods arrive
Save the shipment when you order. When goods physically arrive, expand the card and click Apply to Stock. This updates store inventory and locks in the landed cost for FIFO profit tracking. Can only be done once per shipment.
💡 Save Draft right after ordering — come back to add products and apply stock when goods arrive.
💸 Expenses
1
Add an expense manually
Go to Expenses → Add Expense. Fill in date, amount, description, category, and company (Bee Events, SWITS, or Both).
2
Stock write-offs appear here automatically
When you write off stock (damaged, gift, content etc) in Stock Adjustments, the cost is automatically added to this panel under "Stock Write-offs". No manual entry needed.
3
Import Amazon orders in bulk
On Amazon: Account → Order History Reports → Request report → Download CSV. Then click Import Amazon CSV. All orders come in at once under "Online Purchases".
💡 For shared expenses, select "Both (split equally)" — the system splits it 50/50 in all reports automatically.
✏️ Stock Adjustments
1
Write-off — stock leaving without a sale
Use for damaged goods, gifts, samples, personal use, TikTok content, or stocktake corrections. Select the product, choose a reason, enter the quantity. The cost is calculated via FIFO and recorded as a business expense automatically.
2
Restore Return — resellable goods returned by a customer
First add a deduction line on the original invoice in the invoicing system (this offsets the revenue). Then come here and restore the stock. Units are added back at zero cost — so when you resell them, the full price is profit.
💡 Always write off remaining stock before deleting a product — otherwise the cost disappears from your P&L silently.
🛍 Products
1
Hide or show products in the store
Tap Hide/Show on any product to toggle visibility for both retail and distributor customers at once. Takes effect in the store immediately.
2
Quick write-off shortcut
Tap Write-off on a product to jump straight to Stock Adjustments with that product pre-selected.
3
Delete a product
Tap Delete to permanently remove the product from the store. If stock remains, you will be warned — write off remaining units first for accurate P&L. For editing prices, photos, and sections, go to the store admin.
📊 Product P&L
1
Revenue pulled automatically
Confirmed invoices from the invoicing system appear here instantly. Shopee revenue is added automatically on the 15th of each month.
2
Costs use FIFO (First In, First Out)
When you sell a product, its cost is taken from the oldest unsold batch first. So if you bought 100 units at $1.43 then 100 more at $1.61, the first 100 sales are costed at $1.43. Write-offs also consume the FIFO pool so your remaining stock value is always accurate.
3
See profit per sale and per customer
Expand any product card to see every sale, its exact FIFO cost, and profit — even when selling the same product at different prices to different customers.
📋 P&L Report
1
COGS based on sales, not purchases
Cost of Goods Sold is calculated from units actually sold in the period using FIFO — not the shipment purchase date. A heavy buying month won't show inflated costs if nothing was sold yet.
2
Expenses include write-offs
Stock write-off costs appear as "Stock Write-offs" under Operating Expenses in the P&L breakdown, so your net profit accurately reflects all stock losses.
3
Revenue includes Shopee
Shopee after-fee earnings are included in SWITS revenue automatically. The P&L shows your true total revenue across all channels.
🧾 GST (SWITS only)
Bee Events is not GST-registered. SWITS is GST-registered — 9% GST applies on all SWITS sales.
1
Output Tax = 9% of SWITS revenue
GST collected from customers and held on behalf of IRAS. Includes both B2B invoice revenue and Shopee revenue.
2
Input Tax = GST paid on imports
When logging a shipment, select the applicable GST rate. That amount is tracked as input tax you can offset against output tax.
3
Net GST Payable = Output − Input
Go to GST Summary, select the quarter and year. Always verify with your accountant before filing with IRAS.
📤 Exporting
1
CSV — for Excel or your accountant
Downloads every transaction in full detail including shipments, expenses, and write-offs. Filter by month, year and company first.
2
PDF — formal P&L report
Generates a professional P&L statement. Select period and company, then click Download PDF.
